# Attribution

## Data sources

The current dataset is an original, hand-compiled seed. No third-party dataset
has been imported. Machine-readable provenance lives in
[docs/sources.json](docs/sources.json).

When an external source is imported, add an entry here with its name, URL,
version/commit, retrieval date and license, and any attribution text that
license requires.

## Trademarks

Make and model names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used

manufacturer.
